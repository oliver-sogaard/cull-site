# What's new

## 1.0.0 · 2026-10-20

- The first release. CULL is the step before Lightroom: open a folder of RAW files, judge each frame with one key, and the verdicts are already there when you import.
- Enter keeps a frame, Backspace rejects it, F marks a favorite, the arrows move. Hold Space for the frame at 100 %, at the point the camera focused.
- Frames of one scene are grouped before you reach them, and a burst is boxed as one.
- Nine RAW formats, straight from the card: Canon CR3 and CR2, Sony ARW, Nikon NEF, Fujifilm RAF, Olympus ORF, Panasonic RW2, Leica RWL and DNG. Each frame opens from the preview the camera already wrote.
- A verdict is an XMP sidecar beside the file, or inside a DNG. Lightroom reads it on import. The image data is never rewritten.
- Free culls up to 250 frames in one session, with every feature. CULL Pro culls shoots of any size, one key for two computers.
- Windows and Mac. The app updates itself.
