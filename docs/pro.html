How a license works

  • The key is shown on the page right after payment, and sent by email. In CULL, open Settings → License, paste it and press Add license.
  • A key works on two computers at a time. To move it, remove it on one computer (Settings → License) and enter it on the other.
  • CULL confirms the license online about every twelve hours and keeps working offline for 30 days.
  • A subscription is managed from inside CULL: Settings → License → Manage subscription.
  • Lost the key? Have it sent again.
