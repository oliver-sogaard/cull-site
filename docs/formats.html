Nine RAW formats, straight from the card. Each frame opens from the preview the camera already wrote.

Most cameras save one at full size, so zooming in shows every pixel. Some save a smaller one; then the app zooms as far as that preview goes and says so in a small badge.

What each one gives

Canon
CR3 and CR2. Full-size previews on every camera.
Nikon
NEF. Full-size previews on every camera.
Sony
ARW. Cameras from 2021 on save a full-size preview; older ones a smaller one.
Fujifilm
RAF. Every camera saves a preview at about half size.
Olympus and OM System
ORF. Every camera saves a 3,200-pixel preview.
Panasonic and Leica
RW2 and RWL. Cameras from 2022 on save a full-size preview; older ones a smaller one.
DNG
Whatever wrote the file decides. Cameras that shoot DNG save full size; converters and phones usually save a small one.

What is written, and where

A keep is Lightroom's pick flag, a reject its reject flag, and a favorite a pick with a purple label. They go into an XMP sidecar next to the file. A DNG carries its own metadata and Lightroom ignores a sidecar beside one, so there the verdict goes inside the file. The image data is never changed.

Not supported

Pentax PEF, Samsung SRW, Hasselblad 3FR, Sigma X3F and the old Canon CRW. Most of those cameras can also shoot DNG.
